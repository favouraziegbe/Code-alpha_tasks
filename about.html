MY STORY

More than just the code.

My name is Favour Aziegbe, and I'm a Frontend Developer interested in building thoughtful and functional digital experiences.

My interest in technology comes from a curiosity about how things work and how ideas can be transformed into something useful. Frontend development gives me the opportunity to combine logical thinking, problem-solving and creativity.

I've developed my foundation through structured learning, personal projects and practical experience. My training at BeTechified and my Code Alpha internship have helped me strengthen my understanding of web development and the importance of continuous practice.

I believe good development involves more than making something look appealing. It also means considering usability, accessibility, responsiveness and the experience of the person using it.
